## Notes
- Fact: Bitcoin started 2009 aiming for P2P, censorship-resistant money; 2010 pizza purchase for 10,000 BTC.
- Opinion: crypto "has hit the mainstream" but isn't mature; cites scandals (Hawk Tuah coin, $Libra, ByBit hack).
- Definitions: tokenomics = supply, governance, incentives of a token. Tokenization = blockchain tokens tied to real-world assets, fractional, traded.
- Prediction: tokenization of real estate, commodities, stocks, IP, carbon credits.
- Stated: stablecoin market cap $235B as of March 2025. Stablecoins "have the potential" to cut cross-border costs up to 80% (unsourced).
- Opinion: "Stablecoins are crypto's first product-market-fit." CBDCs = central-bank digital currencies, lack decentralization.
- Predictions: regulation ends the "Wild West"; institutions converge with blockchain; better tokenomics; DAOs as digital corporations.
