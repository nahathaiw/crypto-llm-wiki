## Notes
- An order book = list of pending buy and sell orders on an exchange; used to match orders and determine market prices.
- Bids = buy prices; asks/offers = sell prices; book shows quantity at each price level.
- Continuous price discovery as new orders arrive and are matched.
- Bid-ask spread = highest bid vs lowest ask gap; smaller spread → higher liquidity.
- Liquidity = ease of trading without big price moves. Depth = total volume across price levels.
- Limit order = set a specific price; market order = execute at best current price. Exchange auto-matches a market buy against the best ask (and vice versa).
- Slippage: fill at a different price than expected, due to volatility / thin depth.
- Order books used on centralized and decentralized exchanges; CEXs with order books dominate. DEXs like Uniswap typically use Automated Market Makers (liquidity pools + pricing formula) instead.
- Alternatives: OTC, direct P2P trading (called relatively inefficient for liquidity), atomic swaps.
- Page does NOT define "last traded price".
