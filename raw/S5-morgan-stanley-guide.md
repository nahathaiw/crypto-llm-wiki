## Notes
- Crypto = digital assets on blockchain networks that record/verify transactions without a central authority like a bank.
- Uses: peer-to-peer value transfer; smart contracts (lending, trading, collectibles).
- Bitcoin: largest by market cap, first (2009), created by mining, hard cap 21 million coins; blocks roughly every 10 minutes.
- Public ledger copied on thousands of computers → extremely hard to alter.
- Traditional payments use a trusted intermediary (bank, card network); crypto uses a distributed network of nodes that validates transactions.
- Tens of thousands of cryptocurrencies, a few dominate. Groups: digital currencies, smart-contract platforms, applications.
- Ethereum: a "virtual computer" for smart contracts; fees paid in ether. Solana: fast, low-cost; SOL staked for rewards.
- Tether (USDT): dollar-pegged, ~$1 but can deviate under stress. USDC tracks the US dollar.
- Ways to get exposure: buy on an exchange; rewards; ETPs/ETFs/futures.
- Risks: encryption could be broken (quantum), software bugs, government action, high volatility (more than S&P 500), concentration of holdings, no central recourse if lost/stolen, illegal activity and fraud. Disclosures: not legal tender, no government backing, exchanges/wallets have been hacked or failed, no deposit insurance, one asset fell >50% in a day, uncertain taxes.
- Advice: proceed with caution, get educated.
