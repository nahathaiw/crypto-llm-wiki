## Notes
- Crypto grew fast but its future is uncertain; framed as paradoxes.
- Claims analysts estimate global crypto market will more than triple by 2030 "to a valuation of nearly $5 billion" (cited to CoinDesk). ⚠ Looks inconsistent with trillion-dollar scale market caps elsewhere; flagged in lint.
- Many first-time traders during the pandemic; meme-stock effect.
- US: consumers over 35 are nearly half (47%) of those expecting to invest in crypto in next 6 months (no sample/year given).
- Mining concentrated in big players; a Tesla investment moved BTC price +20% in a day.
- Institutions entering (US Bank custody) — may threaten crypto's independence from traditional finance.
- Investors support regulation (legitimacy, less fraud/volatility) but fear it limits P2P nature/anonymity. They trust payment companies/exchanges more than governments to lead.
- "50 [%?] of crypto investors would be comfortable using it to pay for online shopping" (unit unclear).
## Quotes
- "Its growth has been faster than ever, yet its future has never been so unclear."
- "Investors believe in regulation, yet are worried about many of the impacts that regulation will bring about."
