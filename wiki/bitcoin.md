# Bitcoin (and keys and signatures)

## In one sentence
Bitcoin, launched in 2009, was the first cryptocurrency: a peer-to-peer electronic cash system secured by proof-of-work [S1][S5].

## Key ideas
- Bitcoin is the largest cryptocurrency by market capitalization [S5].
- It has a hard cap of 21 million coins [S5].
- New bitcoins are created by mining. The first transaction in each block gives new coins to the block's creator [S1][S5].
- **Keys and signatures:** "We define an electronic coin as a chain of digital signatures." To pay someone, the owner signs the previous transaction together with the next owner's public key [S1].
- A transaction can combine several inputs and usually has two outputs: the payment and the change sent back to yourself [S1].
- **Privacy:** the public can see amounts moving between public keys but not who owns them. Using a new key pair for each transaction helps [S1].
- Because no central authority manages it, there is limited recourse if coins are lost or stolen [S5].
- A famous early real-world purchase was two pizzas for 10,000 BTC in 2010 [S3][S7].
