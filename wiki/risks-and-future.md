# Risks and the future

## In one sentence
Crypto is very volatile and comes with few safety nets, and its future depends heavily on regulation and adoption, which no one can predict for sure [S5][S6].

## Key ideas
- Crypto "is characterized by significant volatility", more than the S&P 500. One asset fell more than 50% in a single day [S5].
- Crypto is not legal tender and has no government backing [S5].
- If coins are lost or stolen there is limited recourse, and exchanges and wallets have been hacked [S5].
- Other risks include software bugs, government action, concentration of holdings among a few addresses, fraud, and future threats to encryption such as quantum computing [S5].
- Real scandals and hacks have happened, for example the ByBit hack and the $Libra scandal [S7].
- Investors support regulation because they see it as a way to reduce fraud and volatility. They also fear it could limit crypto's peer-to-peer nature and anonymity [S6].
- Institutions such as banks are entering crypto [S6][S7].
- **(opinion/prediction)** Tokenization of real-world assets, such as real estate and stocks, will grow [S7].
- **(opinion/prediction)** The "Wild West" phase is ending as governments regulate [S7].
- Morgan Stanley's advice: "proceed with caution" and get educated first [S5].
