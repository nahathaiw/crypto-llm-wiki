# Ethereum and smart contracts

## In one sentence
"Ethereum is a blockchain with a computer embedded in it": a shared computer (the EVM) that runs programs called smart contracts [S2].

## Key ideas
- Every Ethereum node keeps a copy of the state of one shared computer, the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) [S2].
- **ETH (ether)** is Ethereum's native cryptocurrency. Users pay ETH fees to run transactions. Part of each fee is burned and the rest goes to the validator, and bigger jobs cost more, which prevents spam [S2][S5].
- **Smart contracts** are programs uploaded to the EVM that anyone can call. They work "like a sort of vending machine": when the conditions are met, they act automatically [S2].
- Apps built from smart contracts are called dapps, for example marketplaces, financial tools and games [S2].
- Smart contracts power lending, trading and digital collectibles [S5].
- Ethereum is secured by Proof of Stake (see [[consensus]]) [S2].
- Morgan Stanley groups crypto into digital currencies (like Bitcoin), smart-contract platforms (like Ethereum and Solana), and applications [S5].

## Beginner analogy (AI-written, not from a source)
A vending machine doesn't need a cashier. You insert money and press B4, and the machine follows its rules and gives you the snack. A smart contract does the same with digital assets.
